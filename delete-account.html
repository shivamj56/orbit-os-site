Delete your Orbit account

For Orbit OS: TV Remote & Setup on Google Play (package dev.shiv.orbit), by Shivam Anil Jha.

You only have an Orbit account if you signed in with Google in the app. Using Orbit on your home Wi-Fi without signing in creates no account.

From the app

  1. Open Orbit on your phone.
  2. Open Settings (the gear at the top right of the remote).
  3. Tap Delete my Orbit account.
  4. Choose whether to keep your TV backups in Google Drive (they are deleted unless you untick the box), then confirm.

By email

Send an email from the Google account you signed in with to heyshivm2002@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Orbit account. Everything below is deleted within 30 days, and you get a reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your Orbit account (Firebase Authentication): your email address, name and user ID.
  • Everything stored for you in Orbit's database: your TV's name, its last-online time and state, and any remote commands or answers still waiting.
  • Your TV backups in Google Drive, unless you choose to keep them.

What is kept

  • Nothing is kept on Orbit's side once deletion finishes. There are no logs or analytics to keep.
  • Your TV stays as it is. To put the TV back the way it was, use Undo everything in the app before or after deleting your account.
  • If you kept your Drive backups, they stay in your Drive until you delete them.
