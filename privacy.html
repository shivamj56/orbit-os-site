Orbit OS

Privacy policy

For the Android app Orbit OS: TV Remote & Setup (package dev.shiv.orbit) and the Orbit apps it puts on your TV. Effective 11 November 2026.

In short: Orbit has no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and nothing is sold or shared. Most of what Orbit knows stays on your phone and your TV. Signing in with Google is optional; if you do, a small amount of data goes to Google's Firebase so your phone can reach your TV away from home, and your backups go to your own Google Drive. You can delete all of it from the app.

Orbit OS is made by Shivam Anil Jha, an independent developer in India. On Google Play it is published through the developer account of Om Shri Hari Trading Pvt Ltd. Questions about this policy or your data: heyshivm2002@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone and your TV

These never leave your phone, your TV and your home network, and are not sent to us:

  • Your TV: its name, its address on your Wi-Fi and the pairing token it gave your phone, encrypted with a key in your phone's Android Keystore.
  • The set-up record. Before Orbit changes anything on a TV it writes down the change (which preinstalled apps it switched off, the TV's own home screen, its accessibility and screensaver settings, which Orbit apps it installed), with the TV's model, Android version and app list, on the phone and on the TV, so Undo can put everything back.
  • Your TV's app icons, cached so the remote opens quickly, and your Orbit settings.
  • The name your TV greets you with, stored on the TV.
  • The phone's debugging key. To set up a TV, Orbit uses the TV's own debugging connection on your home network after you press Allow on the TV. The phone makes its own key; the TV keeps only the public half.

To find your TV, Orbit listens for the TV's own announcement on your Wi-Fi and checks your local network for the TV's port. On your Wi-Fi, button presses, what is playing, app icons and, in Help mode, pictures of the TV's screen go straight between the phone and the TV, and the TV answers only phones paired with it. Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for Orbit, so none of this is copied to Google's backups or to a new phone.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no advertising ID, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. Google processes the data above only to provide its services to the app.
  • Orbit does not read your contacts, messages, photos, location, microphone or camera.

Deleting your account and data

  1. In the app, open Settings › From anywhere and tap Delete my Orbit account. With Also delete my TV backups from Google Drive left on, Orbit deletes your backups, unlinks your TV and deletes everything its cloud keeps under your account, deletes your Orbit account, removes its access to your Google account and signs you out. Nothing changes on your TV.
  2. Or email heyshivm2002@gmail.com from your account's address with the subject "Delete my Orbit account". We delete everything within 30 days and reply when it is done.

Smaller steps: Settings › Forget this TV removes the saved TV from the phone; Settings › From anywhere › Unlink TV removes the link; Settings › Backups deletes backups one by one; uninstalling Orbit removes everything it keeps on the phone. You can remove Orbit's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To put a TV back as it was, use Settings › Undo Orbit OS on this TV. See Delete your Orbit account.

Children

Orbit is a tool for adults setting up their own TV. It is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
