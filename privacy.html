Orbit OS

Privacy policy

For the Android app Orbit OS: TV Remote & Setup (package dev.shiv.orbit) and the Orbit apps it puts on your TV. Effective 11 November 2026.

In short: Orbit has no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and nothing is sold or shared. Most of what Orbit knows stays on your phone and your TV. Signing in with Google is optional; if you do, a small amount of data goes to Google's Firebase so your phone can reach your TV away from home, and your backups go to your own Google Drive. You can delete all of it from the app.

Who we are

Orbit OS is made by Shivam Anil Jha, an independent developer in India. On Google Play it is published through the developer account of Om Shri Hari Trading Pvt Ltd. Questions about this policy or your data: heyshivm2002@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone and your TV

These never leave your home network and are not sent to us:

  • Your TV's address and its pairing key. Kept on your phone, in Android's encrypted keystore, so the remote can talk to the TV over your Wi-Fi.
  • The set-up record. Before Orbit changes anything on a TV it writes down the change (which preinstalled apps it switched off, the home screen it replaced, the settings it changed) on the phone and on the TV, so Undo can put everything back.
  • The name your TV greets you with. Stored on the TV.
  • The phone's debugging key. To set up a TV, Orbit uses Android's own debugging connection on your home network. The phone makes its own key for this; the TV keeps only the public half after you allow the phone on the TV's screen.

Remote commands sent over your Wi-Fi go straight from the phone to the TV.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no advertising ID, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. Google processes the data above only to provide its services to the app.
  • Orbit does not read your contacts, messages, photos, location, microphone or camera.

Deleting your account and data

  1. In the app, open Settings and tap Delete my Orbit account. This deletes your data in the Realtime Database and your Orbit account, and, if you leave the box ticked, your TV backups in Google Drive. Nothing changes on your TV.
  2. Or email heyshivm2002@gmail.com from your account's address with the subject "Delete my Orbit account". We delete everything within 30 days and reply when it is done.

You can also remove Orbit's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To undo Orbit on a TV, use Undo everything in the app. See Delete your Orbit account for the details.

Children

Orbit is a tool for adults setting up their own TV. It is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
